# BINAIUI Math

## Relation operator

BINAIUI keeps ordinary arithmetic intact. Canonical BINAIUI equations also use `=` as a relation operator for identity, convergence, or role-equivalence inside the system.

That gives two explicit modes:

```text
Arithmetic: 4 + 4 = 8
BINAIUI:    4 + 4 = X → ∞
```

The second line means two complete four-part systems cross at `X`, and the crossing produces further relation. It does not overwrite arithmetic.
